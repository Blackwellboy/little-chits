  Found on the live save: six stockpiles in each world stood half rebuilt as warehouses for want of stone and cord.
  Only a chit's own home upgrade was ever carried on, the full stores took no stone in, and while one stood no
  more storage was begun: full stores and no warehouse held each other up. Fixed: `builder.store_upgrade_plan()`

  six finished in six days); `actions.village_stores()` is the one definition of usable nearby storage (station
  stores, builder stock, the panel's affordability); the panel shows upgrades under way; warehouses count for
  research and rot like any store (two stale tuples). A/B on top of F5, 12 seeds: 60 days era 7.1 -> 7.8 (9 of 12
  seeds reach the Machine Age: the steam engine), goods made +18%, food x3, copper 10.7 -> 35.1, starvations 0.5 ->
  0.2, goods left on the ground 843 -> 81; 30 days discoveries 42.2 -> 43.4, population 55.7 -> 58.4.
- [x] **F11. Steam engine stall / furnace ore gate.** The long live run exposed four gates; F5 fixed far-station
  reach, next-era input reservation stopped steel being consumed entirely by gears, F10 removed the storage deadlock,
  and this slice closes the remaining ore-supply gate without magic transfer: an eligible chit with a pick physically
  walks to a locally visible or remembered stocked mine and carries ore into a real village store within furnace
  production radius until reserve + two iron batches are local. The distilled regression proves the trip unlocks an
  iron bill, refuses unseen remote infrastructure, respects ore weight, stops when enough is local, and handles the
  final one-unit top-up.

  independently raises Machine-Age conversion.
- [x] **F12. The chief's choice is rarely the model's** (audit 6): of the project moments in `server.log`, about 31
  say "the chief's mind was never asked", 11 "didn't answer" and 8 "chief changed before choosing"; elections come

  changed, fallback) and count it on the live run; only then decide. Don't lengthen the one-day window blindly if
  the cause is the saturated 3090 or election churn.
  **Instrumented** (fix/f12-chief-lifecycle): every question records asked, sent or blocked (the chief's brain
